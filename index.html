Stadium Guidelines

Ticket Verification

Carry your valid ticket while entering the stadium. Tickets will be verified at the entrance.

Visitors must carry a valid government-issued identity proof for verification.

Prohibited Items

Outside food, sharp objects, fireworks and other restricted items are not allowed inside the stadium.
